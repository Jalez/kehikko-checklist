- **Checklists for one thing at a time.** Checklist shows the checklists for exactly one target: the container you picked on the kehikko, or the item or paper passage you picked most recently. The footer names it and says why, such as "For !4233 (picked in References)". When nothing is picked, it tells you how to pick something.
- **One checklist per type of item.** Merge requests, pull requests, issues, tasks and papers each use a single standing checklist, so every item of that type gets the same one. Choose it under "Checklist for merge requests" and the other entries in the header's Checklist menu. Turn on "Choices for this kehikko only" to choose differently on one kehikko.
- **No stacking, no guessing.** Your role on an item no longer changes which standard you see. A type with no checklist chosen says so and offers its starter with one press. Where several standards used to apply to one type, the one used most recently is kept, and the others wait under Manage checklists.
- **How to use.** Checklist explains its rules in the "How to use" window: what it shows, standing versus one-off lists, who can change what, where test steps belong, and what each state means.
