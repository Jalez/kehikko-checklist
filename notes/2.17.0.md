Kind: feature

- **Other modules find your checklists without naming them.** A module that reviews your work can read the checklists held against a change, propose a new one for you to accept, and answer items, by finding "the module that keeps checklists" instead of a fixed name.

## For agents

- Checklist now offers the `checklist.holder` service, naming its tools `checklists`, `create_checklist`, `add_checklist_item`, `hold_checklist` and `check_item`. A module finds it with `services.find` and calls the tools the descriptor names; the rules are unchanged (an agent never changes the person's tick).
