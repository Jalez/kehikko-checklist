- **Standing checklists come first.** A checklist that applies to every pull request, merge request, issue or paper is shown as Standard, ahead of any one-off list, on the item you pick and under Manage checklists.
- **Starters, one press away.** Pull and merge request standards, issue standards and paper standards can be adopted from Manage checklists or the header's Create menu, then reworded or trimmed like any list. Nothing is added until you press.
- **Your standards stay yours.** Agents answer the lines, but they can only create or change a standing checklist when you asked them to, and they are told that test steps belong in Test runs, not here.
- **See an item at a glance.** Each picked item shows what it is, its progress over time, what failed or is blocked and what is being worked on. A short container shows only that, a larger one adds the lines, and the largest adds who answered what and when.
- **Working and blocked.** A line can be marked as being worked on or blocked, and the footer shows the item, its progress, what is failing and what is underway.
