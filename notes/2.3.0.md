- **Checklists for a type of item and a role.** Say a checklist is for merge requests as reviewer, issues for any role, or papers as author, across the project or one epic, and it appears on every such item, including ones opened later.
- **Always the picked item's checklists.** Pick an issue, merge request, pull request or paper and the pane shows every checklist that applies to it, one section per item; with nothing picked it says so in one line instead of listing things to choose from.
- **Read from your angle.** The header's Angle switch reads items as assignee, reviewer, author or any role, and by default uses your own role on each item. Each container remembers its angle.
- **Lines just for one item.** Add items for a single issue, change or paper on top of its checklists; they are ticked like any other line and stay when the other checklists change.
- **Your every-issue and every-change lists carry over.** They now apply to issues, or to merge and pull requests, for any role, with all their ticks.
