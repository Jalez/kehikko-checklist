- **Checklist reads at one row.** In a container one or two rows tall, each item is a single line, such as "gh#105 · 7/12 · 2 failing", with a tiny progress ring. The footer says the same.
- **A progress ring and a slim state bar.** Progress shows as a ring beside a sparkline of how the answers came in, and failing, blocked and the rest show as one stacked bar. Each tile's title and number sit on their own row above the chart.
- **Tooltips you can reach by keyboard.** Hover or focus a chart for a short summary like "7/12 done · 2 failing · 1 blocked". Use the arrow keys to read a single point, such as "3 ticked at 14:02", or a segment, such as "2 failing of 12".
- **Narrow columns stay clean.** Under about 260 pixels a chart drops its axes and gridlines, long names are cut short with the full name on hover, and nothing scrolls sideways.
