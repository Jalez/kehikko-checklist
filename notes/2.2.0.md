- **Your checklists, beside the work they are about.** Make a checklist and hold it against an issue, a pull or merge request, every change, a paper or one heading of it; it appears wherever that work is in front of you.
- **Ticks belong to one piece of work.** The same list held against two changes keeps two separate sets of ticks, so finishing one says nothing about the other.
- **Lists for how a module is used.** A checklist can be held against one module's container on a kehikko, and shows up there in every epic.
- **Follows the parts you pick.** With parts of the epic picked, only the lists that belong to them are shown.
- **Kept in your project.** Checklists and ticks are saved as plain files inside the project they are about, and agents can read and tick them too.
