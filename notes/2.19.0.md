Kind: feature

- **Other modules read your checklists more reliably.** A module that reviews your work now reads the checklists held against a change as data instead of reading the printed list, so a change in wording here cannot make it misread what you ticked.
- **Your checklist folder says what it holds.** A module that shows or records the project's files can now tell that `.kehikot/checklist/` belongs to Checklist and what is in it.

## For agents

- The `checklist.holder` descriptor says `formats: ['json']` (protocol 2.85.0): `checklists` takes `format: 'json'` and answers the protocol's `checklistListsJson` (no `checklist`: `{ lists: [{ id, name, held }] }`, `held` the targets held by hand) or `checklistJson` (with `checklist`, items as answered for `ref` when given: `state` passed/failed/na or null, `byPerson` true for the person's own tick, `by`, `note`). Without `format` the print is unchanged.
- Checklist provides `module.folders` for `.kehikot/checklist/`.
